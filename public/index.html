AC 43.13-1B is the FAA's handbook of acceptable repair methods: 646 pages, written for the person holding the wrench. Ask a question and each pipeline selects five paragraphs from the same 1,758, hands them to the same local model, and prints the page the manual puts the answer on. Only the selection differs, which is what you are comparing.

A embed the question → cosine over 1,758 paragraphs → 5 pages
B BM25 top 30 + cosine top 30 → merge the two lists by rank → 5 pages
C B's top 30 → the model reads all 30 → 5 pages

Then all three send their five pages, one paragraph each, to the same local model. It writes two sentences and names the page it used. The page number is the manual's own, so you can open the book and read it.
